Privacy Policy for Last Time?

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Last Time? is a simple reminder and activity tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app allows you to create and manage activities, dates, notes, reminder intervals, settings, and activity history. This information is stored locally on your device. The developer does not operate an account system or server for storing this information.

Notifications

The app may request permission to send notifications so that it can remind you about activities you have chosen to track. Notification permission can be changed at any time through your device settings.

Advertising

Last Time? displays advertisements using Unity LevelPlay mediation. Advertising services integrated with the app may include ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, and Yandex Mobile Ads.

These advertising providers may automatically process certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, advertising interactions, and related diagnostic or technical information, as necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services. The exact information processed may depend on the user's device, region, privacy choices, and the advertising provider serving an advertisement.

Third-Party Services

The app may use services provided by:

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Ads
  • Unity Ads
  • Yandex Mobile Ads

These third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable privacy laws.

Advertising Identifiers

Advertising services may use the device's advertising identifier when available and permitted by the operating system and applicable privacy settings. Users may reset or restrict the use of advertising identifiers through their device settings.

Children's Privacy

Last Time? is not specifically directed to children. The application is configured as not directed to children for advertising purposes.

Data Security

Activity information created directly within Last Time? is stored locally on the device. Third-party advertising providers are responsible for information processed through their respective advertising services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
